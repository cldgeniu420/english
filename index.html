Britannia English Level Test

Find your CEFR level · A1 – C2

Welcome to the Britannia English Level Test. This quick test contains 30 multiple-choice questions that start with simple everyday grammar and gradually move towards advanced idioms, vocabulary and complex structures.

For every question choose one of four answers. You cannot go back, so take your time and answer honestly — do not use a dictionary or a translator. At the end you will see your score and your estimated level on the Common European Framework of Reference (CEFR): A1, A2, B1, B2, C1 or C2.

The test takes about 10–15 minutes. Good luck!
